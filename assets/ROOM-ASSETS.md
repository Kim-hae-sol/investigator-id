# STEP 04 / room look development

`records-board-v2.jpg` is an AI-generated set-dressing diffuse image (distributed as JPEG quality 88 for transfer size) created with the built-in image_gen tool. It is a fictional analog records board; the illustrated papers and buildings are atmosphere props, not actual HNJ case evidence. It is mapped onto the physical 3D corkboard and lit by the room's lights. The wall, frame, furniture, lamp and camera motion are geometry.

Final prompt:
Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: a physically realistic diffuse/albedo texture for a 3D investigation-room cork evidence board. Generate a straight-on, orthographic, full-bleed rectangular corkboard surface, landscape 2:1 ratio. Entire image is the board surface: no room, no wooden frame, no surrounding wall, no perspective distortion. Warm brown fine-grained cork with an organically layered arrangement of 20-25 small aged ivory typewritten office records, cream handwritten notes, sepia photographic prints of old office buildings, empty streets and architectural details, a folded street map, tiny brass pushpins and pieces of beige tape. Uneven spacing, some slight overlaps and rotations, tactile paper edges and mild curling, authentic fine print that is not prominent and no readable names, no logos. Managed analog archive, elegant cinematic mystery, not horror. Neutral soft diffuse lighting evenly across the board so a 3D warm spotlight can illuminate it later. No people, no portraits, no red strings, no weapons, no badges, no modern UI, no watermark. High photographic material detail and natural color variation, not illustration or low-poly render.

Other room maps reuse assets/pbr's existing CC0 wood, wall and floor surfaces. Rug weave, paper fallback and environment lighting textures are generated locally with Canvas; they make no external requests.
